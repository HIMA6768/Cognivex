# Limitations

This is a pre-model P7 Streamlit prototype, not a production assessment system. It includes five pages and a session-only vehicle-image upload/preview flow. P4 checks allowed suffix/type, non-empty data, configured byte size, actual decode/format, still-image status, configured dimensions/pixels, and safe mode/orientation normalization. P5 adds deterministic blur and extreme-luminance checks. P6 adds schemas, and P7 adds development-only mock adapters. It does not include quality-based resubmission decisions, real classifier/localizer adapters, ONNX runtime integration, a decision policy, routing behavior, production threshold calibration, model evaluation metrics, monitoring integration, or external service access.

Image dimensions in `ThresholdSettings` are provisional development-only defaults. Model confidence is optional and has no calibrated value. Model preprocessing, model choice, model index mappings, metrics, and production thresholds are unknown. Pending AI model handoff.

The configured upload and geometry limits are operational structural safeguards. P5 quality thresholds are **PROVISIONAL ENGINEERING DEFAULTS** selected against controlled fixtures, not calibrated AI or photographic-quality thresholds. Its 4×4 usable-sharp-tile ratio, median luminance, and extreme-pixel ratios reduce false passes from localized sharp graphics and isolated highlights, but do not make the gate watermark-aware or validated for vehicle photographs. P4 explicitly rejects animated images and creates normalized PNG preview bytes; P5 uses that preview but does not establish a future model preprocessing contract. P6's mock/live metadata and routing statuses are contract vocabulary; a `RoutingDecision` can preserve multiple reasons but no routing policy exists. P7 mock labels, scores, and boxes are deterministic integration fixtures marked `mock: true`, not evidence of an available model, accuracy, probability calibration, or policy. No production readiness, accuracy, security, deployment, or operational claims are made. Credentials and real external-service values are intentionally absent. The prototype is decision support only and cannot determine a claim outcome.
